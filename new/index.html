About Me

I have been engaged in digital creativity for more than 6 years. I started as a video editor and videographer, and over the years I have gained solid experience in filming and editing. Later, I became interested in programming and have been actively working as a full-stack developer on Unreal Engine 5, Next.js, Node.js, and in pure programming languages for 3 years. At the same time, a year ago I started doing music production. Now I create modern digital products, atmospheric tracks, and dynamic videos. I value clean architecture, attention to detail, and a high level of user experience. In my free time, I run a technical blog, experiment with generative design, record tutorials on YouTube, compose music, and edit videos. I believe that a true product comes to life when the code works perfectly, the music evokes emotions, and the visuals look powerful and stylish.
